# Naming Research, Round 2: Additional Homebuzz Candidates

Research run on 2026-10-31 as a supplement to
[the first research round](naming_research_2026-10-31.md). This is a
research-only report: no code was renamed, and no domain or account was
registered.

## Executive conclusion

**Keep Brush & Bit as the provisional recommendation (86.6/100).** The best
new name, **Wall & Watt (83.6/100)**, comes close and broadens the category
story from decorating to electrical, but its spoken form can be heard as
“wall and what.”

**Best new alternatives:** Socket & Sill and Frame & Fuse tie at 83.4. Both
have exact `.com`, `.store`, and `.co` names that returned unregistered
responses, no exact business surfaced in the completed searches, and no exact
USPTO mark was observed. Frame & Fuse loses some distinctiveness because the
phrase also appears in structural-engineering writing; Socket & Sill is more
literal but longer.

This round does not produce a candidate that clearly beats Brush & Bit under
the pre-existing criteria. It does, however, produce three credible backups
that score more than 30 points above Homebuzz (53.2). All recommendations
remain first-pass portfolio-demo candidates, not legal clearance. WIPO and
Instagram could not be validated, and a professional trademark search would
still be required before real commercial use.

## Scope and unchanged criteria

The owner's questions were not answered between rounds, so the first report's
defaults still apply: portfolio-demo use; Instagram, X, and GitHub handles;
exact `.com`, `.shop`, `.store`, or `.co` domains; no premium-domain budget;
and no preferred or prohibited style beyond the prompt's exclusions.

The criteria were deliberately not changed after seeing the names:

| Criterion | Weight | Round 2 interpretation |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Distinctiveness | 25 | Avoid exact, adjacent, and confusingly similar uses |
| Relevance | 20 | Communicate broad home projects or carry that meaning easily |
| Spell/pronounce | 15 | Minimize say-it-once ambiguity |
| Domain quality | 15 | Exact `.com` is 5; exact fallback-only is 3 |
| Brand fit | 10 | Work in the existing heavy slate/yellow wordmark |
| Length | 8 | Five at 11 domain characters or fewer; three at 12–16 |
| Handles | 7 | Confirmed exact GitHub/X plus unchecked Instagram scores 4 |

The full 1/3/5 anchors, hard requirements, Homebuzz diagnosis, and criterion
mapping are in the [first-round report](naming_research_2026-10-31.md).

### Businesses, trademarks, handles, and language

- Exact quoted web searches with home, hardware, tool, construction, and
  business modifiers returned no exact trading business for Wall & Watt,
  Socket & Sill, Frame & Fuse, Grout & Gear, Level & Lumen, Socket & Shade,
  Toolfolk, or Fixward. This records what the search returned; it is not a
  guarantee that no unindexed business exists.
- The same search found an active [Dwellmark home-goods store](https://www.dwell-mark.com/)
  and property-directory uses. It also found active Renvera cleaning and home
  services, including [Renvera's own site](https://renvera.lt/en/about/) and
  an [active UK company record](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/14885526).
  Those are hard business-name failures.
- Official [USPTO trademark search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) queries found
  no exact mark for Wall & Watt, Socket & Sill, Frame & Fuse, Grout & Gear,
  Level & Lumen, or Socket & Shade. Broad results did contain live component
  marks such as `SOCKET`, `FUSE`, `WATT`, and `LUMEN`, which is why this is a
  screen rather than clearance. A broad Toolfolk query returned fuzzy results
  but no exact `TOOLFOLK` wordmark was observed.
- [WIPO Global Brand Database](https://branddb.wipo.int/) again presented an
  ALTCHA challenge. WIPO is unchecked for every candidate.
- Exact GitHub and X pages returned the validated not-found response for the
  three leading new names and Toolfolk. Fixward's X page returned occupied.
  Instagram returned the same response for known and random controls, so all
  Instagram results remain unchecked.
- Exact phrase searches with `meaning`, `slang`, and `offensive` found no
  concerning English meaning for the finalists. The material ambiguity is
  spoken **Watt/what** in Wall & Watt. **Frame and fuse** also has an ordinary
  structural-engineering use, reducing distinctiveness rather than causing a
  negative meaning.

## Rename impact

The implementation impact does not materially change by candidate. Any of
the top names would touch the same metadata, wordmark, configuration, seeded
`homebuzz.test` accounts, tests, documentation, repository references, and
possibly the GitHub remote already inventoried in the
[first-round rename-impact section](naming_research_2026-10-31.md#rename-impact).
The estimate remains medium: the code edits are broad but mechanical, while
existing seeded emails and external repository/domain changes need care.

## Recommendation and next steps

1. Keep **Brush & Bit** as the working favorite. Treat **Wall & Watt** as the
   strongest new challenger and **Socket & Sill** as the clearest backup if
   the Watt/what ambiguity is unacceptable.
2. Have two or three people perform an unaided say-it-once test on those three
   names. Record what each person types; do not reveal the spellings first.
3. Manually complete WIPO, Instagram, state business-register, and broader
   common-law searches for the chosen two.
4. If the project may become a real business, commission professional
   trademark clearance in retail class 35 and relevant goods classes 2, 6,
   7, 8, and 9 before buying or announcing anything.
5. Recheck domain and handle status immediately before registration because
   the results are point-in-time observations, not reservations.
